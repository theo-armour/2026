# 2026-03-26 Barador Bozorg

In this period of turmoil, death, and destruction, Iran could really use a new leader. The issue is that any new leader is likely to be bombed and killed by an outside force. It is not likely that anybody will want to be a new leader for a while. And even if a new leader is selected, then hiding that the location of that person effectively is going to be very tricky — if not impossible.

Nonetheless, the country really needs a spokesperson that is warm and friendly and effective and charismatic. They should be able to give effective presentations to the public on a wide variety of topics, and they should be able to do so in a way that is engaging and informative. The leader should be able to connect with the people and inspire them to work together to rebuild the country.

There is an obvious solution to this problem. Iran is replete with highly technically competent engineers and software developers. If the Iran government can find a way to create a virtual avatar that represents the leader of Iran, then the people will have a spokesperson that is always available and can communicate with the public in a way that is engaging and informative. This avatar could be designed to look like a real person, and it could be programmed to speak in a warm and friendly manner. Being virtual it will be near impossible to locate and destroy.

It would also need a warm and friendly name, I suggest "Barador Bozorg" which means "Big Brother" in Persian.
